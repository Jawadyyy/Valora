Link your account in one go

The bot shows your Valorant store in Discord. To do that it needs a one-time handshake with your Riot session — no password, ever. Follow the six steps once and you're done for weeks.

Time ~2 minutes Need a desktop browser Repeat only every few weeks
No password

You stay in control

01SIGN IN

Sign in at account.riotgames.com — tick “Remember me”

Open account.riotgames.com and log in as normal. The one thing that matters: tick “Remember me” before you press sign in. That's what makes Riot hand out a lasting session. Miss it and the link dies in minutes.

02OPEN TOOLS

Press F12 and click the “Network” tab

This opens your browser's developer panel. It looks technical — you only need one tab in it. Click Network at the top and leave the panel open.

03TRIGGER

In the same tab, visit auth.riotgames.com

Type it in the address bar and hit enter. The page will show “An error occurred! Invalid Request.”

OK That error is exactly what should happen. You're not here to read the page — visiting it just fires off a request that carries your session, which is what the next step reads.
04SELECT

Click the auth.riotgames.com row in the list

The Network panel now shows a list of requests. Click the one named auth.riotgames.com — usually the top one. A details pane opens on the side.

05COPY

Find the cookie: row and copy its whole value

In the details pane, scroll to Request Headers. Find the row that starts with cookie: and copy everything after it. It's a long line that looks like the one below — copy all of it.

TIP Right-click the cookie row → Copy value if your browser offers it — that grabs the whole thing cleanly. Otherwise triple-click the value to select the full line before copying.
06PASTE

Run /login in Discord and paste it

Back in the Team HEX server, type /login, paste the whole line into the cookies box, and send. Your reply is private — nobody else in the channel sees it. The bot replies “Account linked.” and you're in.

You're done
